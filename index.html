Gongfu Tea Ritual

The Art of Mindful Brewing

Four steps to honor the leaf and extract rich taste harmony.

1

Warm Vessel

Rinse teapot and cups with boiling water to warm the clay and equalize temperature.

2

Awaken Leaf

Pour hot water over dry leaves and discard immediately. Inhale the fragrant steam.

3

Steep Gently

Infuse for short intervals, letting tightly twisted leaves open gradually in water.

4

Share & Savor

Pour evenly into tasting cups. Observe clarity, inhale bouquet, and sip slowly.
